Q: What is photosynthesis and what are its main stages?

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy in photosynthetic organisms [1]. Light-dependent reactions generate ATP and NADPH and release oxygen from water [2]. The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into carbohydrates [3].

Q: What is the current state of fusion energy research?

Fusion research studies how light nuclei can combine and release energy under suitable conditions [1]. Magnetic and inertial confinement are two major experimental approaches [2]. A scientific energy-gain result is distinct from a power plant delivering net electricity to the grid [3].

Q: How does CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing work at a molecular level?

A guide RNA directs Cas9 toward a complementary DNA sequence near a compatible PAM [1]. Cas9 can cut the DNA strands at the targeted site [2]. Cellular DNA repair can introduce sequence changes or use a supplied repair template [3].
